# Dot product

The **dot product** is a mathematical operation that takes two equal-length sequences of numbers and returns a single scalar: the sum of their element-wise products.

For two vectors $a$ and $b$ of length $n$:

$$\text{dot}(a, b) = a_1 \cdot b_1 + a_2 \cdot b_2 + \dots + a_n \cdot b_n = \sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i \cdot b_i$$
